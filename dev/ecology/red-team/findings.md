| R5-1 | HIGH | CLOSED | Bug   | Joint identifiability collapse: pi0/phi/theta/z form a feedback loop with weak per-cell likelihood contrast | Fixed in commit `2fd4dd5` (rho0Alpha/Beta ESS raised to 480). **Root cause** of the consistent pi0/phi under-shoot observed in all sim3 runs (rep01 pi0=0.44, rep05=0.38, truth-init=0.42, phi=2 test pi0=0.633 phi=0.397 vs truths 0.75 and 4 or 2). `src/mcmc.cpp:4011-4116` (`gibbs_z_sweep_impl`) computes `lp[v] = log P(z=v \| pi0, theta)` and `ll[v]` per cell, then samples from `softmax(lp + beta*ll)`. The prior contribution at z=0 is `log(pi0)`; at z=1, `log(1-pi0)+log(theta)`. **At pi0=0.4, theta=0.5, the prior already weighs P(z=0)=0.4 vs P(z≠0)=0.6** — i.e. cells with weak likelihood signal (which is most of them; see below) flip away from z=0 with 60% probability per Gibbs sweep. After a sweep, the new z-counts feed back into the pi0 MH (case 35, `src/mcmc.cpp:4609-4654`), which uses the full likelihood + Beta(75,25) prior. Beta(75,25) ESS=100 vs **480 z-cells** (nChar=480 = 120 neo + 360 trans, kEco=2 ⇒ 1 z-column) gives the prior only ~17% posterior weight if the likelihood is informative, or **~30% if the per-cell likelihood contrast |ll(z=k)-ll(z=0)| << 1 nat** (which it is for most characters: nTip=12 per ecology, baseRate=1, branch lengths sub-1 ⇒ per-character signal across the {z=0,1,2} ternary is sub-nat). Combined with the LogNormal(0,1) prior on phi which strongly pulls phi → 1 (prior density at phi=0.4 is **4× higher** than at phi=2, **8× higher** than at phi=4), the chain has a stable attractor near (pi0≈0.4, phi≈1, theta≈0.5, most z≠0). This is consistent with the truth-init run drifting from pi0=0.75 to pi0=0.42 despite starting at truth: the gradient at truth points away from truth. **Per-cell likelihood signal is too weak relative to the priors+data scale; the Gibbs z chooses z by prior probability not data, and pi0 then chases the z-counts.** Proposed fixes (any one, with tests on Sim 2 recovery): (a) raise pi0 prior ESS from 100 to ≥nChar (e.g. Beta(360, 120) for nChar=480 ⇒ ESS=480) so prior cannot be overwhelmed by feedback; (b) raise sigmaPhi from 1.0 to ≥1.5 so phi prior allows phi=4 with comparable weight to phi=1; (c) marginalize z analytically (sum over the 3 categorical values) when computing the per-cell likelihood contribution — turns the per-cell decision into a hyperparameter update on (pi0, theta, phi) only, breaking the discrete feedback loop; (d) collapsed Gibbs: integrate out pi0 and theta given the current z (conjugate Beta-Multinomial), eliminating their MH steps and the feedback. Option (c) is the principled fix and is standard for spike-and-slab models when slab indicators are weakly identified. |
| R5-2 | MED  | CLOSED | Bug   | Phi prior LogNormal(0, 1) is too tight to allow phi=4 truth | Fixed in commit `2fd4dd5` (sigmaPhi 1.0 → 1.5). `R/MkPrimeModel.R:194` defaults `sigmaPhi = 1`. Prior density at phi=4 is `dlnorm(4, 0, 1) = 0.044`; at phi=1 (no effect) it is `dlnorm(1, 0, 1) = 0.399`. **The prior is 9× more concentrated at the null than at truth.** Combined with the symmetry phi ↔ 1/phi (handled post-hoc by `RelabelEcology`), the chain has equal-mass minima at phi=4 and phi=1/4, with a saddle near phi=1 where the prior is concentrated. When the per-character likelihood contrast (see R5-1) is too weak to overcome the prior, phi gets stuck near 1. The phi=2 test reports posterior phi median 0.397 — i.e. the chain settled into the reflected mode (phi<1) but with phi pulled toward 1 from below; after `RelabelEcology` this would map to 1/0.397 ≈ 2.52, but the value reported (0.397) suggests `RelabelEcology` was either not applied or the reflection logic differs from what is documented. Either way, the phi prior is the primary force, not the likelihood. Fix: change default to `sigmaPhi = 1.5` (which puts dlnorm(4, 0, 1.5) = 0.057 within 2× of dlnorm(1) = 0.266, an attainable balance) OR document that the prior is intentionally null-favouring and remove phi=4 from sim3 as biologically implausible. Note: the L-5 / B5 / R4C-6 family of findings on the hard-coded ecology marginal rate 1.0 in `compute_ecology_node_marginals` further depresses the apparent rate signal — phi inference is fighting against a 1.0 constraint at the ecology level. |
| R5-3 | MED  | CLOSED | Bug   | Theta prior Beta(1, 1) is uniform and provides zero counterweight to z-cell counts driving label-switching mush | Fixed in commit `2fd4dd5` (thetaAlpha/Beta 1/1 → 2/2). `R/MkPrimeModel.R:192-193` defaults `thetaAlpha = thetaBeta = 1` (uniform). ## Closing protocol

When a bug is fixed:
1. Update `Status: CLOSED`.
2. Reference the fix commit in the row.
3. Leave the row in the table (so future reviewers see what was found and where).

When a bug is deferred:
1. Update `Status: DEFERRED` and add a short reason.
2. Reference any tracking issue or upstream work.

When a finding turns out to be a false alarm:
1. Update `Status: DISMISSED` and add a one-line reason.
