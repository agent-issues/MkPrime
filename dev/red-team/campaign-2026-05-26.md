`tree_length` and `rate_log_sd` now PASS in all 6 arms. Remaining Mk'
FAILs:
- `kPrime_pooled` is **structurally untestable** within the current
  inference prior `kPrime ~ kObs + Geo(p)` (data-dependent floor). Both
  option α (kSim=2) and option γ (kSim=kTrue, rank in u=kPrime−2) hit dual
  failure modes: α piles ranks at L because binary data → posterior at 2
  but kTrue > 2; γ piles ranks at 0 because on small trees kObs=kTrue ~80%
  of the time, forcing kPrime_post ≥ kObs = kTrue. **Resolution:**
  excluded from the v9 SBC suite; see new finding `SBC-KPRIME-STRUCTURAL`.
- `p` FAIL attribution corrected: v8's `p FAIL p≈3e-6` in geometric arms
  was a structural sister of SBC-KPRIME-STRUCTURAL, not just the kSim=2
  feedback loop. The Gibbs update `p_post ~ Beta(n+1, sum(u_i)+1)`
  collapses to ≈Beta(n+1, 1) (mean ≈ 1) whenever most chars satisfy
  kObs=kTrue (so u_i=0). At 8 tips with geometric prior favoring small
  kTrue, this is still the common case in v9. **`p` may again FAIL in v9**;
  treat as structural, document, do not re-investigate.
