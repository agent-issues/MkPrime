| T-006 | P0 | **APPLIED (2026-05-19), VERIFIED 26.6 % on top of T-005 (30.5 % vs baseline)** | [Optimise] | Tip-edge fast path in `persite_impl` exploits one-hot child CL | `src/mcmc_likelihood.cpp` (`persite_impl` template — `tipChild` branch before the internal-child propagation). **Insight:** when `ch <= nTip`, the child CL for each character is one-hot for the observed state (`clCh[s]=1`, rest 0) or uniform-1 for missing data. `sum_cl` is therefore 1 (known) or K (missing) by construction — no need to load and sum the K clCh entries. The per-state update collapses: known → K writes of `p_diff` + 1 overwrite of `p_same`; missing init → K writes of 1.0 (the JC marginalisation identity `K·p_diff + (p_same − p_diff) = 1`); missing multiply → no-op (×1). **Verified by micro-bench** (5 runs each, Sun2018 EG production-weight workload, 800 iter): T-005-only median **16.45 s** → T-005+T-006 median **12.07 s** (mean 12.07, sd 0.084). **Δ = 26.6 % on top of T-005, 30.5 % vs baseline** (baseline median 17.39 s). The savings compound multiplicatively with K, so the Gibbs k′ sweep (K up to 50) sees the biggest absolute gain. **Tests:** 234/234 across `filter="likelihood|gibbs|ascertain|mcmc-engine"`.
