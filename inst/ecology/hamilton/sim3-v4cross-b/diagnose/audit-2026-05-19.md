## TL;DR — verdict

**The mode-trap claim is a scoring artefact. It does not survive.**

Under root-invariant scoring there is exactly **one** unrooted topology in
the entire 243-sample chain, and clade B is monophyletic in 100 % of
samples (not 34 %). The legacy `prop.part`-based scorer's "contiguous block
1..83 then B-broken from 84 onwards" is a relabelling of which root config
the sampler is currently sitting on, nothing more. The two phi/ll
populations the previous diagnostic identified are two stationary
distributions of root position — not two topology modes.

## Numbers

### B-monophyly time series

|                       | Legacy (prop.part) | Corrected (Splits) |
|-----------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| B-mono samples        | 83                 | 243                |
| B-broken samples      | 160                | 0                  |
| Block structure (RLE) | TRUE×83, FALSE×160 | TRUE×243           |

The "contiguous block 1..83" is a single uninterrupted block of TRUEs
followed by 160 FALSEs, **for the legacy scorer only**. The corrected
scorer returns TRUE for every sample. 160 / 243 samples disagree
between the two scorers; all disagreements are "legacy says broken,
corrected says monophyletic". Zero disagreements in the reverse direction.

### Unique topology count

- Legacy field `topo_hash` (stored in the result `samples` matrix): **14 unique values**.
- Root-invariant Splits-based topology hash: **1 unique value**.

So the "14 unique topologies" claim is also a scoring artefact — the
14 hashes are 14 different ROOT POSITIONS on the same unrooted tree.
There are exactly 10 distinct canonicalised root edges in the chain
(see table below); some collapse to the same `topo_hash` because
`topo_hash` is computed pre-canonicalisation, but the unrooted topology
never changes.

The legacy scorer fails exactly when the root edge cuts ACROSS the B|rest
split (root inside B side, treated as NA by `RootInsideSet`). In all 160
disagreement cases the unrooted tree contains the B-monophyletic split,
but `prop.part` returns the complement and `setequal(p, splitSet)`
returns FALSE.

### Phi / ll bimodality is correlated with ROOT, not topology

LEGACY conditional (the original diagnostic):
- B-mono (n=83):    phi.med = 6.36, ll.mean = -1583.5
- B-broken (n=160): phi.med = 2.95, ll.mean = -1587.8

CORRECTED conditional:
- B-mono (n=243):   phi.med = 3.43, ll.mean = -1586.4 (only one class)
- B-broken (n=0):   N/A

So phi DOES show bimodality across the chain (the marginal distribution
is bimodal), but the bimodality lines up with root configuration, not
with monophyly. Whether this root-correlated phi bimodality is itself
a problem (e.g. label-degenerate root proposal coupled to phi) is a
separate question, but it is NOT a topology mode-trap.

## What survives, what doesn't

- "B-broken from sample 84 onwards" — **does not survive**. Corrected
  scoring shows B-monophyletic in all 243 samples.
- "Mode-trap (H2)" — **does not survive**. There is one topology mode
  in the posterior.
- "14 unique topologies" — **does not survive**. 1 unrooted topology;
  the 14 `topo_hash` values are root-position relabellings.
- Bimodal phi marginal — **survives as a description** but its
  interpretation changes: it tracks root configuration, not clade
  membership. Whether sampler mixing of root position is itself
  pathological for this chain is a downstream question, not a
  mode-trap.

## Project-narrative implications

1. The v4cross-b aware chain has not been shown to be mode-trapped or
   pathological in terms of topology. The original diagnosis was driven
   entirely by `prop.part`'s root-dependence on a single unrooted tree.
2. Any v4cross-b "asymmetry" narrative in the paper draft that builds
   on B-monophyly = 0.34 needs replacing with the corrected score
   (B-monophyly = 1.0). The same caveat already noted for v4cross-b-pt5
   rep01 in the earlier rescore pass applies here too.
3. The phi vs root-config correlation is the genuinely interesting
   residual signal and deserves its own focused diagnostic if we want
   to claim anything about phi mixing on this chain.
